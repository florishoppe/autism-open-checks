## Limitations to state

- GP-recorded challenging behaviour samples only the events that reach primary care.
- Over-the-counter laxatives and incomplete adherence bias the result towards no effect.
- Refills show that treatment continued, not that it worked.
- The design cannot separate pain relief from other effects of treatment, such as better sleep.
